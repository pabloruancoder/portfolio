Our story

A neighbourhood table, ten years in the making.

Sálvia started as a tiny six-table room with one wood oven and a simple idea: cook what's good right now, and cook it with care. A decade later the idea hasn't changed — only the queue out front has.

We work with a handful of local farms and fishers, write the menu each morning, and make our pasta, bread and ice cream in-house. Nothing fancy. Just honest food, generously served.

— Marco & Elena, owners

Meet the chef

Cooking that tastes like somewhere.

Chef Elena Rossi trained across Liguria and Lisbon before bringing her market-led cooking home. Her food is rooted in memory — the coast, the garden, a grandmother's Sunday table.

"Good cooking isn't complicated. It's paying attention — to the season, the fire, and the people at the table."
Elena Rossi
Chef & co-owner
